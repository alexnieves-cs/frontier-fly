humanoid.glb in this directory is a subset packed for a civilian search-and-rescue
simulation. It contains the Rig_Medium mannequin mesh and five clips only:
Idle_A, Walking_A, Sit_Chair_Idle, Sit_Floor_Idle, and Lie_Idle.
Hit, death, and other combat clips from the pack are not included.

The source clips and mannequin are KayKit CC0 assets. They were read from the
CC0 redistribution at https://github.com/NoLimitNexus/game-models
(library/animations/Rig_Medium_*.glb and tools/reference/Mannequin_Medium.glb),
which credits Kay Lousberg for the skeleton, mesh, and animation clips.
